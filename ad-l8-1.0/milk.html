M!LK

The five-member idol group "M!LK". The group's name contains the meaning of "an entity that can be dyed any color." With a concept of versatility, they deliver "happiness, energy, and smiles" in a variety of genres straight to their fans, "Milkys." While she has expanded her activities beyond music to include TV dramas, movies, stage performances, modeling, and variety shows, the contrast between her work and the dazzling performances she puts on at live shows is what makes her so appealing. In 2024, the 10th anniversary of their formation, the group will hold an arena tour including two days at Saitama Super Arena and Osaka-jo Hall, attracting 40,000 people, the largest number in M!LK history. They are rushing forward at top speed towards their goal of a Dome tour.
