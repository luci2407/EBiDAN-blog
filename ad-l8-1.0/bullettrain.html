BULLET TRAIN (ChouTokkyuu/超特急)

On December 25, 2011, the group was formed with the unique style of "main dancer & backing vocals". The group has always provided entertainment and buzz, and in 2022, marking their 10th anniversary, they will be holding an extremely unusual member audition. Four new members were added, bringing the group to nine members. Their live performances, which are highly entertaining and full of unity with the "8th Car" (the name of their fans), have become a hot topic, and tickets for their solo shows always sell out in seconds! They have also taken on the challenge of variety shows, and each member has worked in dramas, movies, and stage productions, as well as directing their own live shows, making the most of their areas of expertise and playing an active role in a wide variety of genres. Keep an eye on BULLET TRAIN Train, whose popularity is on the rise!
